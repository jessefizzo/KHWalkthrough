SFX Audition

Assign the extracted system sounds to the tracker's seven interface voices. Pick a role, then click a sound to hear it and bind it. The current bindings are my best guess from waveform shape alone — attack time, duration and whether the pitch rises or falls — so expect to change some.

1 · Load the bank

Served over http this loads on its own. Opened as a file, pick the System Sound Effects folder (nothing is uploaded — it is read in the page).

2 · Choose a role, then click sounds

3 · Sounds

Sorted shortest first — menu blips live at the top.

4 · Apply

Saves your bindings into this browser, trimmed of leading silence and normalised. The tracker picks them up immediately — they override both the synth and the sounds/ folder.
